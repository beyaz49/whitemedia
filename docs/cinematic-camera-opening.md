# White Media camera opening: production brief

Status: original visual direction prepared; final motion film is still required.
This component is a draft and is intentionally not imported into Home until the film is generated and visually reviewed.

## Visual direction

Low-key real product cinematography in a restrained graphite studio. Sony A7 IV, matte magnesium body, textured grip, genuine metal E-mount, black FE 35mm f/1.4 GM lens, subtle coated optical glass. Narrow white softbox reflections, deep blacks, restrained grain. Preserve White Media's existing black and white identity.

The image generated in the current conversation on 19 October 2026 is the opening-frame reference. It shows the separated Sony A7 IV body and lens in a dark studio. It is a still, not a completed motion film.

## Motion brief

Create one uninterrupted 4-to-5-second high-end product-commercial shot using the supplied opening frame as a strict appearance and lighting reference. Camera body, controls, mount and lens remain rigid and anatomically consistent throughout. The lens rear flange moves along the actual optical axis into the body's bayonet, seats at the flange, and turns a small clockwise locking rotation. No sideways collage, no crossfades between different cameras, no morphing, no floating extra rings, no elastic toy motion. Give the object a controlled, weighted movement.

Start with a tight three-quarter view of the machined mount and lens. A subtle camera truck and slow dolly back reveal their true parallax while the lens seats. End on the fully assembled Sony A7 IV in a composed three-quarter product shot. White softbox highlights travel naturally over the rubber and metal. Keep the scene quiet, tactile and physically credible; no flashy transition, flare burst, sci-fi lights, hands, lettering or generated logo. Finish with a gentle fade toward black so the website can reveal its own existing White Media wordmark.
