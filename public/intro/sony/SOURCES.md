Photography and product marks belong to Sony. The two `*-original.jpg` files are the original downloaded JPEG bytes.

For the moving assembly, `body-empty-angled.webp` is an image-edited, higher-resolution camera body made from both Sony reference photographs. The matching assembled image was generated from that edited body and the original mounted photograph. `lens-isolated.webp` is a transparent lens layer extracted from the difference between those matching frames and refined to remove background pixels. These derivatives are photorealistic edits, not original Sony files or a mechanical 3D model.

The lens contact, bayonet friction and lock sound are original synthesized Foley, not a recording supplied by Sony. “Sesi aç ve filmi izle” starts the film with sound through the visitor's gesture. If untouched, the silent version begins automatically after a short pause and offers a sound replay control.
